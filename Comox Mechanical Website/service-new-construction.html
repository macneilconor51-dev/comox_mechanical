Complete Plumbing Solutions for New Builds

When it comes to new construction, your plumbing system is the foundation of your building's comfort and functionality. At Comox Mechanical, we specialize in designing and installing complete plumbing systems that meet all building codes, exceed industry standards, and stand the test of time.

Our New Construction Services Include:

  • Complete system design and planning
  • Rough-in plumbing installation
  • Water supply line installation
  • Drain, waste, and vent (DWV) systems
  • Fixture installation and finishing
  • Gas line installation and hookups
  • Water heater installation (tank or tankless)
  • Bathroom and kitchen plumbing
  • Laundry room plumbing
  • Outdoor plumbing and hose bibs

Our Process

1. Consultation & Planning: We review your building plans and specifications, providing expert input on the most efficient plumbing layout.

2. Material Selection: We help you choose the right fixtures, pipes, and systems that fit your budget and performance needs.

3. Rough-In Installation: Our team installs all plumbing infrastructure during the framing stage, coordinating with other trades for seamless integration.

4. Inspection & Testing: We conduct thorough pressure tests and coordinate required inspections to ensure everything meets code.

5. Finish Work: Once interior finishes are complete, we install all fixtures, appliances, and accessories with precision and care.
