Tank vs. Tankless: Which is Right for You?

Traditional Tank Water Heaters

Best For: Homes with moderate hot water needs and budget constraints.

  • Lower upfront cost
  • Simple, proven technology
  • Easy to repair and maintain
  • Available in various sizes (40-80+ gallons)
  • Familiar to most homeowners

Lifespan: 10-15 years with proper maintenance

Tankless Water Heaters

Best For: Homes seeking energy efficiency and endless hot water.

  • Endless hot water supply
  • 20-40% more energy efficient
  • Compact, space-saving design
  • Longer lifespan
  • Lower operating costs

Lifespan: 20+ years with proper maintenance

Signs You Need Water Heater Service:

  • No hot water or inconsistent temperatures
  • Strange noises (rumbling, popping, banging)
  • Water around the base of the tank
  • Rusty or discolored hot water
  • Unit is 10+ years old
  • Increased energy bills
  • Not enough hot water for your household
  • Pilot light won't stay lit (gas heaters)

Maintenance Tips

Regular maintenance extends the life of your water heater and maintains efficiency:

  • Flush tank annually to remove sediment buildup
  • Test pressure relief valve yearly
  • Check anode rod every 2-3 years
  • Insulate older tanks to improve efficiency
  • Schedule professional inspections
