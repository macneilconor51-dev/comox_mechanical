Radiant Infloor Heating

Experience ultimate comfort with energy-efficient radiant floor heating systems

The Most Comfortable Heating Available

Radiant infloor heating is widely considered the most comfortable and efficient heating system available. By warming the floor from beneath, heat radiates evenly upward through the room, eliminating cold spots and drafts. Whether you're building a new home or upgrading your current heating, radiant floor heating offers unmatched comfort, energy efficiency, and silent operation. Walk barefoot on warm floors throughout your home and enjoy lower energy bills.

🔥 Superior Comfort

Even, consistent warmth from floor to ceiling. No cold spots, no drafts, no forced air blowing dust around.

💰 Energy Efficient

Up to 30% more efficient than forced-air systems. Radiant heat warms people and objects directly, requiring lower temperatures.

🤫 Silent Operation

No noisy furnace or blowing air. Completely silent heating means a more peaceful home environment.

💨 Better Air Quality

No forced air means no dust, allergens, or pollutants being circulated throughout your home.

🏠 More Space

No bulky radiators, baseboard heaters, or visible vents. Enjoy complete freedom in furniture placement and design.

🎯 Zone Control

Heat different rooms to different temperatures for maximum comfort and efficiency.

Hydronic vs. Electric Systems

There are two main types of radiant floor heating systems. We'll help you choose the best option for your home and budget.

💧 Hydronic (Water-Based)

Warm water circulates through PEX tubing installed beneath your floor, heated by a boiler or water heater.

Best For:

  • Whole-home heating
  • New construction
  • Large areas (500+ sq ft)
  • Long-term energy savings

Advantages:

  • Most energy efficient option
  • Lower operating costs
  • Can integrate with other systems
  • Works with multiple heat sources

⚡ Electric

Electric heating cables or mats installed beneath flooring, controlled by a thermostat.

Best For:

  • Smaller areas (bathrooms, kitchens)
  • Renovation projects
  • Supplemental heating
  • Lower upfront cost

Advantages:

  • Lower installation cost
  • Easier to install in existing homes
  • No boiler required
  • Fast warm-up time

Compatible Flooring Types

Radiant floor heating works with most flooring types, though some conduct heat better than others:

  • Tile & Stone (Best): Excellent heat conductors that retain warmth beautifully
  • Engineered Hardwood (Great): More stable than solid wood, works very well with radiant heat
  • Laminate (Good): Compatible with proper underlayment designed for radiant systems
  • Vinyl & LVP (Good): Works well, choose heat-rated products for best results
  • Carpet (Okay): Works but reduces efficiency; keep total R-value below 2.0
  • Solid Hardwood (Limited): Can work with proper installation and moisture control
