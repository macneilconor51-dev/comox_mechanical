Before We Arrive: Emergency Tips

  • Shut off the water: If you have a major leak, locate your main water shutoff valve and turn it off
  • Contain water: Use towels, buckets, or a mop to contain water and prevent it from spreading
  • Turn off water heater: If you've shut off water, also turn off your water heater to prevent damage
  • Document damage: Take photos for insurance purposes if there's significant water damage
  • Gas leak safety: If you smell gas, evacuate immediately, don't use any electronics or switches, and call us from outside
  • Clear the area: Remove valuables and belongings from the affected area if possible
