Fixture Upgrades

Modernize your space with stylish, efficient fixtures and professional installation.

Transform Your Space with New Fixtures

Upgrading your plumbing fixtures is one of the most effective ways to refresh your bathroom or kitchen. New fixtures not only enhance the aesthetic appeal of your space but can also improve water efficiency, functionality, and overall comfort. At Comox Mechanical, we specialize in professional fixture installation that combines style with performance.

Fixtures We Install:

  • Kitchen faucets and sinks
  • Bathroom faucets and sinks
  • Toilets (standard, low-flow, and dual-flush)
  • Showers and shower systems
  • Bathtubs and soaking tubs
  • Bathroom vanities
  • Garbage disposals
  • Pot fillers and bar sinks
  • Bidets and bidet attachments
  • Water filtration systems
  • Laundry room fixtures

Benefits of Upgrading Your Fixtures

💧 Water Efficiency

Modern fixtures use significantly less water, reducing your utility bills and environmental impact.

✨ Enhanced Style

Update your space with contemporary designs that match your personal aesthetic.

🔧 Improved Function

Newer fixtures offer better performance, easier operation, and advanced features.

🏠 Increased Value

Quality fixtures enhance your home's appeal and can increase resale value.

Popular Fixture Upgrades

Kitchen Faucets

Modern kitchen faucets offer features like pull-down sprayers, touchless operation, and multiple spray patterns. We can help you choose a faucet that matches your kitchen's style while providing the functionality you need.

Bathroom Faucets

From widespread to single-handle designs, we install faucets in various styles and finishes including chrome, brushed nickel, bronze, and matte black. Upgrade to water-saving aerators without sacrificing water pressure.

Toilets

Replace old, inefficient toilets with modern low-flow or dual-flush models that use less water per flush. Enjoy features like soft-close lids, elongated bowls, and comfort-height designs.

Shower Systems

Transform your daily shower with rainfall showerheads, handheld sprayers, body jets, and thermostatic controls. We can create a spa-like experience in your own bathroom.

Sinks & Vanities

Update your bathroom or kitchen with new sinks in various materials and styles — undermount, vessel, farmhouse, and more. We handle complete vanity installations including plumbing hookups.

Our Installation Process

1. Consultation: We discuss your style preferences, budget, and functional needs.

2. Selection: We can provide guidance on fixture selection or work with fixtures you've already purchased.

3. Preparation: We protect your space and prepare the installation area, ensuring proper shutoff of water.

4. Installation: We remove old fixtures, install new ones with precision, and ensure all connections are watertight.

5. Testing & Cleanup: We test everything thoroughly and leave your space clean and ready to use.

Why Professional Installation Matters

While some fixtures may seem simple to install, professional installation ensures:

  • Proper sealing to prevent leaks
  • Correct connections that meet code
  • Optimal water pressure and flow
  • Long-lasting, reliable performance
  • Protection of your warranty
  • Clean, professional finish

Water-Saving Fixtures

We're proud to install WaterSense certified fixtures that meet EPA standards for efficiency:

  • Low-flow faucets save up to 30% water
  • Efficient showerheads use 2.0 GPM or less
  • Dual-flush toilets use as little as 0.8 GPF
  • Smart fixtures with auto-shutoff features

These upgrades can save thousands of gallons of water per year while maintaining excellent performance.
