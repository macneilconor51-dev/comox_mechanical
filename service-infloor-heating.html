Radiant Infloor Heating

Experience ultimate comfort with efficient, evenly distributed warmth throughout your space.

The Most Comfortable Heat You'll Ever Experience

Radiant infloor heating provides luxury comfort and energy efficiency that traditional heating systems simply can't match. By warming your floors and radiating heat upward, this system creates an even, comfortable temperature throughout your space while operating more efficiently than forced-air systems. Whether you're building new or renovating, Comox Mechanical can design and install the perfect radiant heating system for your home or business.

🔥 Why Choose Radiant Infloor Heating?

Imagine stepping onto warm floors on a cold winter morning, or enjoying consistent warmth throughout your entire home without drafts or cold spots. That's the radiant heating advantage.

Benefits of Radiant Infloor Heating

🌡️ Superior Comfort

Even heat distribution eliminates cold spots and drafts. Warm floors feel luxurious and inviting.

💰 Energy Efficient

Uses 15-40% less energy than forced-air systems. Operates at lower temperatures while maintaining comfort.

🤫 Silent Operation

No noisy furnaces or blowing air. Enjoy peaceful, quiet warmth throughout your space.

🌬️ Better Air Quality

Doesn't circulate dust, allergens, or dry air like forced-air systems. Ideal for allergy sufferers.

🏠 Design Flexibility

No bulky radiators or vents to limit furniture placement. Clean, unobtrusive heating.

⏰ Zone Control

Heat different areas to different temperatures for maximum comfort and efficiency.

How Radiant Infloor Heating Works

Radiant heating uses a network of tubing installed beneath your flooring. Warm water circulates through the tubing, heating the floor surface which then radiates warmth upward into the room. This creates consistent, comfortable heat from the ground up — exactly where you need it most.

The system is controlled by thermostats that allow you to set specific temperatures for different zones in your home. Because heat rises naturally from the floor, the system operates efficiently and maintains consistent comfort levels.

Compatible Flooring Types

Radiant heating works beautifully with various flooring materials:

  • Tile & Stone: Excellent heat conductors, retain warmth beautifully
  • Concrete: Perfect thermal mass for efficient heat distribution
  • Engineered Hardwood: Compatible with proper installation techniques
  • Laminate: Works well when properly specified
  • Vinyl & Linoleum: Good conductivity and compatibility

Note: Solid hardwood is generally not recommended due to expansion/contraction issues. We can advise on the best flooring choices for your project.

New Construction vs. Retrofit

New Construction

The ideal time to install radiant heating is during new construction or major renovation. We'll design the system to integrate seamlessly with your floor assembly, ensuring optimal performance and efficiency.

Retrofit Installation

Radiant heating can be added to existing homes during renovations, particularly when replacing flooring. While more complex than new construction installation, the comfort and efficiency benefits make it worthwhile.

System Components

  • High-efficiency boiler or water heater
  • Circulation pump and manifolds
  • PEX tubing network beneath flooring
  • Zone controls and thermostats
  • Insulation to maximize efficiency
  • Mixing valves for temperature control

Our Installation Process

1. Consultation & Design: We assess your space, discuss your needs, and create a custom system design.

2. Engineering: We calculate heat loads, design tubing layout, and specify equipment for optimal performance.

3. Installation: Our experienced team installs insulation, tubing, manifolds, and all system components.

4. Testing: We pressure test the system to ensure no leaks before covering with flooring.

5. Commissioning: After flooring installation, we start up the system, balance zones, and ensure proper operation.

Energy Savings

Radiant heating typically operates at lower temperatures (85-140°F water temp) compared to traditional radiators or baseboard heaters. This efficiency translates to:

  • Lower monthly heating bills
  • Reduced energy consumption
  • Smaller carbon footprint
  • Quick return on investment

Maintenance

Radiant infloor heating requires minimal maintenance:

  • Annual boiler/water heater inspection
  • Periodic system pressure check
  • Pump and component inspection

With proper installation, systems can last 30-50 years or more with minimal service needs.
