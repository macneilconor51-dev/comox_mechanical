What to Do in a Plumbing Emergency

Step 1: Stay Calm

Take a deep breath. While plumbing emergencies are stressful, staying calm helps you take the right actions.

Step 2: Stop the Water (If Possible)

Locate and shut off the water supply:

  • For isolated issues: Turn off the fixture's shut-off valve
  • For major problems: Shut off the main water valve to your home
  • Know where your main shut-off is located before an emergency happens

Step 3: Minimize Damage

  • Move valuables and furniture away from water
  • Place buckets under leaks
  • Use towels to soak up standing water
  • Turn off electricity if water is near electrical outlets or appliances

Step 4: Call Comox Mechanical

Call us immediately at (778) 992-6324. Provide details about the emergency so we can prepare to help you effectively.

Common Emergency Situations We Handle

Burst Pipes

Whether caused by freezing temperatures, age, or pressure issues, burst pipes can cause significant water damage quickly. We'll locate the problem, stop the leak, and repair or replace damaged sections.

Sewage Backups

Sewage backups are not only unpleasant but also pose serious health risks. We respond quickly to clear blockages and restore proper drainage.

Water Heater Failures

Especially critical in winter, a failed water heater needs immediate attention. We diagnose the issue and repair or replace your unit as needed.

Major Leaks

From slab leaks to pipe connections, we use advanced detection methods to locate and repair leaks before they cause extensive damage.

Frozen Pipes

Winter brings the risk of frozen pipes. We can safely thaw frozen pipes and repair any that have burst, plus provide prevention advice.

Preventing Plumbing Emergencies

While we're always here for emergencies, prevention is the best approach:

  • Schedule regular plumbing inspections
  • Know the location of your main water shut-off valve
  • Insulate pipes in unheated areas during winter
  • Avoid putting grease, coffee grounds, or harsh chemicals down drains
  • Replace old plumbing systems before they fail
  • Address small leaks before they become big problems
  • Have your sewer line inspected if your home is over 20 years old
